Our Philosophy

“To know is to judge without hatred,” said Simone de Beauvoir.

The AI transition is not merely technical. It is a shift in place, reference points, values, and relationships.

Our approach: to transmit, with honesty and care, the information you need to make truly informed choices.

Neither tool nor enemy

AI is often reduced to two caricatures: “deployment” or “combat.”
Instead, we treat it as a stage to be approached with lucidity and responsibility within a continuous process.

To do so, we help you:

  • Reduce emotional and cultural noise to regain clear decision-making.
  • Avoid ideology: neither panic nor naïveté.
  • Remain human without becoming “anti-future.”
  • Coexist.

Relationships first

The primary impact of AI first appears in our relationships with others and with ourselves:
trust, professional identity, sense of usefulness, cohesion.

This is where conflict arises — and where stability can be rebuilt.


Sharing knowledge is the greatest wealth. Benefit from our expertise.
