For harmonious integration and a peaceful relationship with artificial intelligence.


In our interventions, we do not treat AI as a tool to deploy or fight, but as a human and biological rupture to navigate.

Rather than opposing humans and AI, we emphasize what they share to help you face the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence with greater serenity.

What we do

We help individuals, teams, and organizations understand the revolution underway, in order to reduce relational friction between humans and AI, and make clear-minded decisions.

Our role:
  • Clarify, defuse, and restore meaning when emotional saturation occurs.
  • Support responsible human–AI coexistence (without naïveté, without panic).
  • Anticipate co-evolution trajectories and their concrete impacts.
