Our Philosophy

“To know is to judge without hatred,” wrote Simone de Beauvoir.

The AI transition is not merely technical. It reshapes our place in the world, our reference points, our values, and our relationships.

Our approach is simple: to share, with honesty and care, the insights you need to make truly informed decisions.

Neither tool nor enemy

AI is often reduced to two caricatures: “deployment” or “combat.”
We instead approach it as a stage to navigate with lucidity and responsibility, within an ongoing human process.

We help you:

  • Reduce emotional and cultural noise to restore clear decision-making.
  • Avoid ideology: neither panic nor naïveté.
  • Remain human without becoming “anti-future.”
  • Learn to coexist.

Relationships first

The primary impact of AI appears first in our relationships with others and with ourselves:
trust, professional identity, sense of usefulness, and cohesion.

This is where conflict emerges — and where stability can be rebuilt.


Sharing knowledge is the greatest wealth.
